Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

Overview

The AutoLogin Extension ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our approach to privacy and how we handle your data.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage Only

All your credentials and settings are:

  • Stored exclusively on your local device
  • Encrypted before storage using strong encryption
  • Never transmitted to external servers
  • Accessible only through your browser

Data Collection Policy

We maintain a strict no-data-collection policy:

  • No personal information collection
  • No tracking of user behavior or activities
  • No cookies or tracking technologies
  • No analytics or usage statistics
  • No data sharing with third parties

Host Permissions

The extension requires broad host permissions (http://*/* and https://*/*) for:

  • Enabling user-selected website automation
  • Supporting dynamic login functionality across various domains
  • Allowing credential capture on any user-trusted website

Important: These permissions are only used when explicitly triggered by user action.

Security Measures

We implement several security measures to protect your data:

  • Strong encryption for stored credentials
  • User-triggered automation only - no automatic form submission
  • Optional master password protection
  • Open-source code for security transparency

Updates to Privacy Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be:

  • Posted on this page
  • Announced through the Chrome Web Store
  • Always maintaining or increasing privacy protection
